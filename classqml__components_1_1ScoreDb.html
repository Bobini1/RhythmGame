Detailed Description

Provides cached results and asynchronous full scores for one profile.

Results are loaded into memory when the profile opens and refreshed after saves and imports. chartResults and courseResults update QML bindings automatically, without SQL or replay decoding when read. Result objects keep their identity and live as long as this profile's ScoreDb. Imports update their properties in place. Deleted runs leave the maps, but retained objects remain readable until the profile closes. Full-score queries execute in the thread pool. Each returned PendingReply owns its cancellation; callers retain and cancel only the work they started. Full scores returned to QML live independently of their reply. For C++-only queries, score objects are children of the reply and share its lifetime.

◆ getScore()

support::PendingReply * qml_components::ScoreDb::getScore ( QObject * result)

Loads a BmsScore or BmsScoreCourse for a result from this profile. Concurrent requests share decoding but have independent cancellation and object lifetimes. Decoded lists share their backing storage. An idle cache retains up to 64 MiB of estimated payload; live consumers may retain more. Missing records and malformed attachments fail the reply. Imported scores may succeed without a recording or gauge history. ScoreDetails supplies a declarative QML interface to this operation.
