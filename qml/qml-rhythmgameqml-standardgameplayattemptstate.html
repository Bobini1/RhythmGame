    Detailed Description

    Use this component when writing your own gameplay exit or result flow. StandardGameplayFlow already includes it.

    Bind gameplay to the supplied context. A hit sets attempted unless its judgement is Poor, EmptyPoor, MineHit or MineAvoided. Changing the context or returning the current chart to ChartRunner.Ready resets the flag. The component observes both players when a second player is present.
