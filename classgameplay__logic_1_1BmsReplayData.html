QList< int > gameplay_logic::BmsReplayData::earlyTimingCounts

Timing buckets: Perfect, Great, Good, Bad, Poor, EmptyPoor. Negative deviation is early; zero and positive deviation are late. Calculated from the loaded replay, not stored in the score database.
